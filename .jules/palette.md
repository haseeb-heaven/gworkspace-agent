## 2024-05-24 - Visual Hierarchy in Gradio Buttons
**Learning:** When Gradio buttons are placed side-by-side without variants, primary actions visually blend with secondary actions, leading to higher cognitive load and potential misclicks.
**Action:** Always assign `variant='primary'` to main action buttons (e.g., 'Run', 'Authenticate') when placed near secondary buttons to establish clear visual hierarchy.
