## 2024-05-19 - Adding Primary Variants to Gradio Buttons
**Learning:** Adding `variant="primary"` to key action buttons (like "Run" or "Authenticate") significantly improves visual hierarchy, especially when placed near secondary actions. Gradio's default button styling can sometimes blend together, making the primary CTA less obvious.
**Action:** Always verify that primary action buttons in Gradio interfaces are visually distinct from secondary buttons by utilizing the `variant` parameter appropriately.
